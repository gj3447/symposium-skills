# Prometheus method grounding

The useful methodological core is simple: reduce avoidable action under uncertainty by gathering direct,
traceable evidence first. Diverse axes help expose blind spots only when they examine different evidence or
failure modes. More agents or findings do not imply a truer answer.

The method separates:

- observation from inference;
- discovery from independent confirmation;
- coverage from evidential weight;
- contradiction from mere wording variation;
- a local research result from canonical ratification.

For scientific T2 work, Popperian falsifiability, explicit null/multiplicity treatment, dependency-aware
Bayes, and checkpoint-scoped Lakatos analysis are conditional gates defined by the ICE science-feedback
policy. They are not mandatory decorations for ordinary engineering or exact algebra.

Naesengmoon supplies adversarial review, Jaebaeman bounded orchestration, and Longinus code-to-claim
binding. Prometheus does not duplicate or automatically invoke those methods.
