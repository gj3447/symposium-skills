# Prometheus adversarial checks

Use these checks when research informs a material decision:

- steelman the target and competing explanations;
- seek a direct counterexample or falsifier;
- verify source versions, dates, and quoted context;
- distinguish independent corroboration from shared-input repetition;
- test the strongest decision-critical calculation or runtime claim;
- expose selection effects, missing controls, and excluded alternatives;
- preserve supported dissent and unknowns.

Formal multi-lens review is a separate `$taliban` invocation when risk warrants it. It is never triggered
by a fixed finding count. One supported blocker can control the decision; unsupported objections remain
notes rather than votes.
