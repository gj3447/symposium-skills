# Prometheus gates

The gates are evidence checks, not KG writes or count thresholds.

## G0 Target

The exact question, decision, scope, evidence bar, and stop condition are frozen.

## G1 Source

Decision-critical claims have inspectable provenance and primary/version-matched sources when available.

## G2 Independence

Shared inputs and derivative reports are grouped. Repetition of the same evidence is not counted as
independent corroboration.

## G3 Contradiction

Counterevidence and supported dissent remain visible. A strong counterexample is not averaged away.

## G4 Applicability

The evidence actually bears on the frozen target. Missing or invalid null models are reported as
`NOT_APPLICABLE` or `NOT_ASSESSED`, not forced into a numerology verdict.

## G5 Decision

The conclusion is no stronger than the evidence. `INCONCLUSIVE` is acceptable.

## G6 Persistence

Ordinary output stays local. A reusable/material proposal remains `PENDING` until separately ratified.

Failure at a gate yields a bounded missing-evidence item. It does not automatically create a Lesson,
ActionPlan, seed, or recursive research cycle.
