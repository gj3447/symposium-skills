# APT adversarial review

Formal adversarial review is conditional on target risk or explicit request. Freeze the target, contract,
oracle, lenses, and blocker conditions. The executor and reviewer are distinct when independence is
material.

Reviewers should steelman the artifact, test boundary/failure cases, inspect direct evidence, and preserve
supported dissent. A finding needs a falsifiable claim, provenance, applicability, and limitation.

One evidence-backed blocker can block; unsupported objections gain no force from repetition. Zero findings
requires a record of checks and limits but does not force invented nitpicks. Use `$taliban` for a formal
multi-lens receipt. No review automatically creates feedback nodes, Lessons, or recursive rounds.
