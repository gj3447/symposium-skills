# APT methodological grounding

APT's useful core is staged refinement with explicit artifacts: intent becomes bounded responsibilities,
contracts become executable behavior, and evidence constrains each transition. The phases are neither a
claim about natural science nor proof that every project needs every ceremony.

The method separates:

- semantics from implementation;
- decomposition from dependency;
- specification from evidence;
- execution from independent review;
- local learning from canonical persistence.

Aristotelian, Hegelian, Lakatosian, Fristonian, OODA, and compiler analogies can illuminate parts of the
workflow but do not create runtime requirements. In particular, Lakatos applies only to a declared
programme/fiber checkpoint with longitudinal evidence, and numerical Bayes applies only with explicit
hypotheses, a frozen prior, both likelihoods, and dependence/selection handling.

Historical theoretical material and former mandatory automation remain in `references/_legacy/` and the
repository's theory archive; they are not active execution instructions.
