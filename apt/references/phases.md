# APT phase contracts

## SA — semantic anchor

Captures objective, actors, domain, scope, constraints, assumptions, provenance, and unresolved questions.
It preserves user-primary language and labels interpretation.

## SP — span decomposition

Produces cohesive bounded units with responsibilities, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and owners.
Decomposition follows semantics and risk rather than fixed line or child counts.

## ST — contract refinement

Defines typed inputs/outputs, preconditions, postconditions, invariants, failure cases, examples, and the
evidence that would satisfy or falsify the contract.

## SCW — implementation

Implements the smallest approved slice within exact write authority, with directly relevant checks and
preservation of foreign dirty state. Runtime effects remain behind explicit boundaries.

## Cleanup

Removes duplication and accidental structure while preserving verified behavior. Tool and metric counts
are diagnostic only.

## MetaReview

Runs only for an explicit or material reusable method issue. Routine cycles end without a Lesson. A child
phase or research task is a separate bounded invocation, not an automatic transition.
