# TPA safety and evidence rules

1. Freeze the exact target revision and inventory scope before recovery.
2. Prefer parser/AST evidence over grep-only structural claims.
3. Distinguish explicit contracts from inferred conventions.
4. Pattern claims list required elements, counterevidence, and alternatives.
5. Fix drift denominators and exclusions before measurement.
6. Default subagents and phases to read-only; writes require exact authority.
7. Preserve partial results, conflicts, and supported dissent.
8. Use directly relevant tests, builds, traces, or exact readbacks for runtime claims.
9. Reviewer, pattern, symbol, and drift counts are telemetry, not verdicts.
10. One supported blocker may block; missing evidence yields `INCONCLUSIVE`.
11. Discovery is a bounded candidate, not automatic research or phase re-entry.
12. Local recovery artifacts are default; canonical changes require qualified `PENDING` evidence and
    separate authorized ratification.

Historical automatic KG, status, seed, ActionPlan, Lesson, and recursive feedback rules are inactive.
