# TPA evidence gates

- Exact target revision, scope, exclusions, and denominator are frozen.
- Inventory claims use parser/AST evidence where available.
- Contract records distinguish explicit from inferred convention.
- Pattern records list required elements, counterevidence, alternatives, and confidence basis.
- Anchor/binding output is a proposal until separately authorized.
- Drift measurements report numerator, denominator, exclusions, evidence, and impact.
- Independent review is conditional on material risk or policy.

One supported blocker may block. Counts are coverage telemetry. Missing evidence yields `RETURN` or
`INCONCLUSIVE`, not an automatic status mutation or critic sprint.
