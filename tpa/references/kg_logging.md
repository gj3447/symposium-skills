# TPA local receipts and persistence

Normal TPA work produces a local recovery bundle, not a KG write. Record exact target revision, artifacts,
commands/parsers, evidence lineage, gaps, drift denominators, decisions, and actor/date.

Material reusable evidence may become a provenance-bearing `PENDING` proposal. Repetition/high risk can
justify a pending verdict but does not establish a root cause. A Lesson candidate requires evidenced cause
and reusable prevention.

Canonical anchor, binding, status, confidence, supersession, or ActionPlan changes require an identified
pending ID and separately authorized ratifier/writer with before/after exact readback. Independent review
or reproduction is evidence, not self-ratification. No executable mutating Cypher belongs in a normal TPA
phase receipt.
