# Naesengmoon validation checklist

- [ ] Exact target, revision, decision, scope, and blocker criteria were frozen.
- [ ] The target was steelmanned against its primary context.
- [ ] Lenses cover distinct risks and have bounded task specs.
- [ ] Decision-relevant findings have direct evidence and provenance.
- [ ] Shared inputs and derivative reports are identified.
- [ ] Supported dissent and coverage gaps are preserved.
- [ ] The verdict follows declared criteria, not counts or unanimity.
- [ ] `APPROVE` has no unresolved supported blocker.
- [ ] Missing evidence yields `CONDITIONAL` or `INCONCLUSIVE`.
- [ ] The receipt stayed local or became only a qualified `PENDING` proposal.
- [ ] No unauthorized mutation or automatic follow-up occurred.
