# Naesengmoon gates

## Target gate

The target revision and acceptance/blocking criteria are exact.

## Steelman gate

The review addresses the intended claim or contract rather than a weaker substitute.

## Evidence gate

Every decision-relevant finding has an inspectable claim, provenance, and applicability assessment.

## Independence gate

Executor/reviewer relationship and shared evidence lineages are explicit. Repetition is not independent
corroboration.

## Blocker gate

One supported in-scope blocker is sufficient to block. Unsupported objections and headcount are not.

## Approval gate

All declared blocker conditions were checked or shown not applicable, and no supported blocker remains.
Otherwise return `CONDITIONAL` or `INCONCLUSIVE`.

## Persistence gate

The receipt is local unless evidence meets the material/reusable threshold. A `PENDING` proposal cannot
self-ratify or mutate canon, status, confidence, or configuration.
