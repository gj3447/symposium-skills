# Naesengmoon phases

The authoritative workflow is [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md).

## Phase 0 — freeze

Identify the exact target/revision, decision, contract, oracle, scope, lens budget, independence, and stop
condition before review.

## Phase 1 — steelman

State the strongest faithful interpretation and verify source context. Ambiguity is recorded before it is
classified as a defect.

## Phase 2 — lens plan

Choose only lenses that cover distinct failure modes. Each lens gets one bounded read-only task with an
observable blocker condition and structured output.

## Phase 3 — evidence

Use direct oracles where possible. Findings without inspectable claims and evidence are rejected. Preserve
supported dissent and dependence between results.

## Phase 4 — synthesis

Decide `APPROVE`, `CONDITIONAL`, `BLOCK`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` from evidence and declared criteria. Reviewer
counts and unanimity do not determine the verdict.

## Phase 5 — receipt

Return a local receipt. Material reusable evidence may become a `PENDING` proposal. Remediation,
ratification, MCTS, and follow-up review are separate authorized tasks.
