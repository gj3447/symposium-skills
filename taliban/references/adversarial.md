# Naesengmoon adversarial practice

Useful adversarial moves include:

- produce the strongest counterexample allowed by the target contract;
- test boundary, malformed, empty, timeout, cancellation, and partial-failure cases;
- compare claims with exact code, formulas, primary sources, or runtime behavior;
- identify assumptions hidden in defaults, data selection, environment, or shared inputs;
- ask what observation would reverse the proposed verdict;
- distinguish a missing check from a demonstrated defect;
- preserve a supported minority objection.

Defect injection is optional and must be safe, reversible, and authorized. Do not create findings merely
to meet a quota. A lens that finds no defect reports the checks actually performed and their limits.
