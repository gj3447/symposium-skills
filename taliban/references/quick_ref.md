# Naesengmoon quick reference

```text
freeze target + contract + oracle
  -> steelman
  -> choose distinct bounded lenses
  -> gather direct evidence
  -> preserve dissent and dependence
  -> decide from blocker conditions
  -> local receipt or qualified PENDING proposal
```

- One supported blocker may block.
- Zero findings is neither automatic pass nor automatic rejection.
- Lens/reviewer counts measure coverage only.
- Missing oracle or scope yields `INCONCLUSIVE`.
- No automatic KG write, Lesson, MCTS, sibling, or recursion.
