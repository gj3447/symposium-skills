# Naesengmoon method grounding

The Rashomon metaphor denotes multiple defensible perspectives on one target. Its engineering value is
heterogeneous failure-mode coverage, not majority rule. Independent lenses reduce shared blind spots only
when their evidence and assumptions differ.

The method therefore separates:

- steelmanning from objection;
- coverage from evidential weight;
- corroboration from shared-source repetition;
- a missing check from a falsified claim;
- local validation from canonical ratification.

Scientific T2 work additionally follows the ICE target/fiber, null/multiplicity, evidence-axis, Bayes, and
Lakatos gates when applicable. Naesengmoon does not infer a programme verdict from reviewer counts.
